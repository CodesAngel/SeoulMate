K-drama Wrapped: current and proposed

Today the Taste profile page analyses taste (a persona, a genre radar, favourite actors) and shows four "Marathon Stats" boxes, but nothing tells a fan about their year. The proposal adds a yearly summary told as six full-screen slides, ending in an image fans can post. Every number is counted from the fan's list and ratings; nothing is generated. The sample below is a real set of 23 catalog dramas, and the numbers on the slides were computed from them.

1. The slides like Spotify Wrapped

  • 23 dramas = list items marked Completed with updated_at in 2026.
  • 392 episodes = the episodes of those dramas (plus episodes watched on dramas still Watching, once that's saved; see Details).
  • 448 hours = episodes × each drama's duration ("1 hr. 10 min." → 70 min; 60 if blank).
  • Behind the numbers: a tilted wall of the year's posters.
  • Click the card, the arrows or Next to go on.
  • Most-watched actor = the actor billed 1st–3rd most often in those dramas.
  • In this sample Lee Jong Suk and Park Bo Young tie at 3. Ties go to the one whose dramas the fan rated higher, and the slide says so.
  • Favourite trope = the story tag found in most of the fan's dramas, weighted so common tags don't always win (the same IDF weighting used for similar-drama scores).
  • In this sample: Nice Male Lead (8 of 23), then Friendship (6), Healing and Bromance (5).
  • Genre mix: genre counts, top 5, as bars.
  • Fan type is a fixed rule, not a model: "The Romantic" (romance in 60%+ of dramas), "The Binger" (200+ hours), "The Sleuth" (thriller or mystery 40%+), and a few more. This sample is 87% romance.
  • Top-rated = the fan's highest score in ratings this year. Ties go to the drama finished first.
  • The 10/10 for Reply 1988 is sample data.
  • The share card is the downloadable image (1080 × 1350, drawn here at 4:5).
  • Save image downloads it; Copy link makes a public snapshot page whose preview in chats is this same image.
  • The snapshot holds only these numbers, never the fan's live list, and the owner can delete it.
  • Fewer than 3 dramas finished this year: Wrapped stays locked, with a progress bar and a link to My list.
  • From December, the Taste profile page shows a banner above Marathon Stats. Home could show the same banner for a week.

What changes

  1. A new page, /wrapped/2026, signed in only. On a phone the slides fill the screen (no site header); on a computer they sit in a 400 × 720 card with arrows on both sides, and ← → keys work.
  2. Six slides, each one number with a short sentence: totals, top actor, top trope, genre mix and fan type, top-rated drama, share card. A progress bar at the top shows where you are.
  3. Always dark, in the site's plum colours (--c-mulberry #2D1738 into the dark theme's paper), whatever theme the fan uses. The page around the card follows the theme.
  4. A share image and a public snapshot page, /wrapped/s/{id}.
  5. A banner on the Taste profile page from 1 December, linking to it.

Details

  • Needed first: the episode tracker saves progress in the browser only (localStorage). Move it to the database (new column watchlist_items.episodes_watched, saved through the existing watchlist PUT). Without that, episodes and hours count finished dramas only. The airing calendar (feature 6) needs the same column.
  • API: GET /me/wrapped?year=2026 returns the stats as JSON, computed on request.
  • Sharing: POST /me/wrapped/share saves a snapshot to a new table, wrapped_shares (id, user_id, year, stats_json, created_at).
  • Share image: Next's image response API (read the Next 16 docs in node_modules/next/dist/docs/ first). The same image is the snapshot page's og:image.
  • "Completed this year" uses updated_at. If a fan edits an old item in 2026, it would count as finished in 2026. A separate completed_at column set when the status becomes Completed fixes that.

Cost

  • One query over the fan's list and ratings (tens of rows) plus catalog fields already in memory on the server: milliseconds.
  • The share image is drawn once per share and cached.
  • No search or ranking change.
  • Size M. To be useful this year it must be live by about 1 December. Most fans joined recently, so the first year is a soft launch.

To decide

  1. Use updated_at for "finished this year", or add completed_at first?
  2. Six slides, or fewer (for example, drop the top-rated slide)?
  3. Which fan types, and should the share card show the fan's display name or stay anonymous?
  4. Ship for 1 December, or wait until next year when there is more history?
