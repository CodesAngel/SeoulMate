Six degrees of K-drama: current and proposed

Today the only link between actors is the cast row on a drama page, and it leads to a search. The proposal is a small daily puzzle: link two actors through dramas they share, in as few steps as possible. Fans pick from menus that only offer real links, so they can't make a wrong step; the challenge is finding a short route. Every chain in the mock is real catalog data, found with the same search the game would use.

1. The puzzle page like "Six Degrees of Kevin Bacon"

  • Step 1 is done: Park Bo Young was in Gold Land with Kim Sung Cheol.
  • Step 2's drama menu is open with Kim Sung Cheol's real dramas (8 in the catalog).
  • Click "Arthdal Chronicles Part 3": Song Joong Ki is in its cast, so the chain closes. Any other drama leads to the longer result.
  • The chain is drawn as people and posters. On a phone it runs top to bottom.
  • Green squares, one per step, for the share text.
  • A real 3-step route: Oh My Ghost → Jo Jung Suk → Hospital Playlist → Shin Hyun Been → Reborn Rich.
  • The extra step shows as an amber square, and the shortest chain is shown underneath.
  • Giving up shows the shortest chain and ends today's streak. Free play is still open.

What changes

  1. A new page, /connect: today's pair at the top with the best possible number of steps, then the chain the fan builds, one row per step: Drama menu (the current actor's dramas, with posters and years) and With co-star menu (that drama's main cast).
  2. If the target actor is in the chosen drama, the chain closes by itself; no extra click.
  3. Result card: green when the fan matched the shortest chain, amber when longer (with the shortest shown), grey after giving up.
  4. Free play in the side column: any two actors, answered instantly ("Kim Go Eun → Lee Min Ho: 1 step, The King: Eternal Monarch").
  5. Names link to actor pages (feature 1), so the puzzle doubles as a way to browse.

Details

  • The graph: actors and dramas, linked when an actor is billed 1st–6th (so links go through real roles, not cameos). About 3,300 actors and 15,600 credits.
  • Shortest path: breadth-first search in memory, in a new small module backend/connect.py, built at startup from meta.pkl (already loaded).
  • Endpoints: GET /connect/daily (today's pair and the best length) and GET /connect/path?from=&to= ("no link within 6 steps" when there's none).
  • Daily pair: chosen by a date seed from actors with 5+ dramas whose shortest chain is 2–4 steps.
  • No new tables: daily results and streaks use localStorage, like the quiz.

Cost

  • The graph adds a few MB of memory to the API; a search takes under 10 ms.
  • No search or ranking change.
  • Size S, about one session, once actor pages exist (it reuses their links).

To decide

  1. Main cast only (billed 1st–6th), or include supporting roles for more routes?
  2. A daily pair plus free play, or free play only at first?
